Overview
This is an innovative product designed to help those affected by Complex Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder (C-PTSD) rekindle the love and support they may have missed out on. The delightful, table-top robot features a simple "Answer" button that initiates a five-question dialogue, helping users express their concerns. It then connects users to "The Best Parents' App," a community of experienced parents ready to provide empathetic solutions.

Every parent loves their child deeply, but sometimes they struggle to express it effectively or understand the right methods to offer support. This project aims to bridge this gap by connecting C-PTSD patients with the world's most compassionate parents through a meaningful medium.

👋Background
C-PTSD on Children Who Grew Up In Depressed Education And Family Environment

The social anxiety and psychological issues in contemporary young adults do not solely originate from the pressures of life and work. Apart from individuals who deliberately opt for solitude, these concerns may also be intricately linked to the interaction patterns fostered within the suppressive educational culture of China's indigenous families, further compounded by the presence of intergenerational trauma. Consequently, these individuals may already be afflicted with C-PTSD, albeit unbeknownst to themselves.

Symptons of C-PTSD

Complex post-traumatic stress disorder (C-PTSD) is caused by ongoing trauma which lasts for months or years and result in borderline personality disorder such as emotional flashbacks, toxic shame, self-abandonment,  vicious inner critic, and social anxiety.

Differentiation & Effect

Inability to self-argue

Background Image Ideas

In order to infuse a sense of “Towards rebirth from the brink of death” into the scene, I use dreamy colors to depict somber elements representing the meaning behind the five questions.

The 'Nightmare' captures the aftermath of the incident with eerie visuals, expressing both the yearning for an escape and its harmful effects.

The 'Devil' scene uses a dark illustration to show the underlying reason of the incident and offers a visual metaphor for the user's grief that is both beautiful and terrifying.

The "Injured" sequence, which depicts the internal wounds caused by the incident, presents a moving image of behavioral difficulties and emotional difficulty. There's pyrotechnics instead of blood, signifying the bilateral effects of pain.

The 'Escape' scene demonstrates the acts performed, signifying the user's attempt to escape the consequences of the incident, but being stuck at a dead end due to inappropriate behavior.

The 'Indulge' scenario artfully employs vibrant visuals of submerging into water surrounded by goldfish and moonlight, symbolizing a metaphorical salvation that not only captures the gravity of the situation but also imbues the viewer with a nuanced understanding of the profound and optimistic aspects of the experience.

Information Architecture

The patient's bot's information architecture is carefully designed into five stages, which correspond to the sequential steps of a medical consultation. This allows users to experience an intuitive journey that is similar to visiting a doctor.

Interactions - Reflection Stage

The interface design entails clicking on bubbles to dynamically increase words because the screen is too small to allow for typing. This visually accentuates and encourages users to investigate and consider the primary themes of their emotional experiences.

💡Conclusion
Key Takeaways
Tracing Origins

Nowadays, most of the design researches only focus on how to solve the problem rather than figuring out the cause of the problem. This project not only analyze patients' origin but also guide them on a journey to understand the roots of their experiences.

Contextualization (The Power of Language)

This project employs metaphorical narratives within the five questions, providing users with a rich and symbolic framework for self-reflection. This unique approach not only enhances personal introspection but also integrates responses from the "best parents' app" into a meaningful and resonant exploration of the user's journey.

Beneficiary & Stakeholder

It is unique that the roles are interdependent and mutually influential for this project. Patients find healing through parents and professionals, who, in turn, benefit from patient reflections. Simultaneously, parents collaborating within the ecosystem not only support each other but also contribute significantly to the healing of patients.

Landing Feasibility

It goes beyond the conceptual by meticulously planning the release at each step, ensuring a feasible and practical deployment that transforms the project into a concrete reality. It is important to consider and test the landing feasibility for other projects in the future.
